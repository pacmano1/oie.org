Eclipse Wordmark Review

Logo review · Draft for approval · 1 October 2026

A new wordmark for Eclipse Open Integration Engine™

The project is now formally named Eclipse Open Integration Engine, so the words in the logo need to say so. The symbol stays exactly as it is. Below are four options for the lettering, shown side by side and then one at a time.

How to respond

For the Steering Committee and maintainers. Reply in the project Discord with:

  1. Your choice of wordmark: A, B, C or D.
  2. Your view on the trademark sign for the small app icons (see Small icons): yes, no, or large sizes only.

Why the logo is changing

The project entered incubation at the Eclipse Foundation on 7 September 2026, and its formal name became Eclipse Open Integration Engine (announcement). Two rules in the Eclipse Project Handbook apply to the logo.

“The primary branding for any project name is fully-qualified formal name which includes the brand (typically Eclipse …) prefix.” Eclipse Project Handbook, Formal Name
“For a project’s official logo, the designer must ensure that it includes a small trademark (™) or registered trademark (®) symbol (as appropriate) in the graphic or immediately adjacent to it.” Eclipse Project Handbook, Logos and Graphics

None of today’s logo files carry a trademark sign, so every option adds one.

Eclipse does not require a typeface for project logos. The Handbook encourages projects “to create a unique logo”. The Foundation’s own brand guidelines (September 2025) set Inter as its typeface, which is why options C and D are here.

Putting the Eclipse Foundation’s logo inside a project logo needs permission from its Board of Directors. None of these options do that. They use the word only.

What changes and what stays

The symbol, its three colours and both layouts (the wide banner and the stacked version) stay as they are. The words become “Eclipse Open Integration Engine™”. In the stacked version, “Eclipse Open” shares the first line, which keeps the three lines close in width. The symbol-only files gain a small ™.

Each option in detail

Each option on a light and a dark background. The points listed are things to weigh, not a ranking.

A

Raleway SemiBold

Today's typeface and weight, all in navy.

  • Same typeface and weight as today's lettering, so the existing words look exactly as they do now.
  • openintegrationengine.org sets all of its text in Raleway, so the logo and the website match.
  • No visual link to the Eclipse Foundation's own typeface.
B

Raleway SemiBold, orange "Eclipse"

As A, with "Eclipse" in the orange already used in the symbol.

  • Everything in A, plus "Eclipse" picked out in the symbol's orange (#e2690e).
  • That orange is close to the Eclipse Foundation's brand orange (#F06C02), so the word reads as a nod to Eclipse.
  • A two-colour wordmark. Wherever the logo is printed in one colour, the emphasis disappears.
C

Inter Bold

The Eclipse Foundation's typeface, at the weight its guidelines give for headlines.

  • Matches the typeface the Eclipse Foundation uses for its own materials.
  • Heavier and wider than today's lettering.
  • openintegrationengine.org uses Raleway, including the project name in its header right beside the symbol. The two would differ unless the website changes fonts too.
D

Inter SemiBold

The Eclipse Foundation's typeface, one weight lighter than C.

  • Same typeface as C, one step lighter. Closer to how heavy today's lettering looks.
  • Inter's letters are a little wider and plainer than Raleway's.
  • Same website point as C: the site's Raleway header text would differ from the logo unless the site changes too.

In the Swing Administrator

The Administrator’s main window shows a single-colour version of the banner in its top-right corner, on the title bar. That bar is blue (#2A75B2) by default, and administrators can change its colour in Settings, so this version is plain white on a transparent background. The login window shows the full-colour banner. Today both are 215 × 30 px images.

The 215 × 30 px slot

The engine expects these images at 215 × 30 px (BrandingConstants.java). Adding “Eclipse” makes the banner longer, so the engine has to either make room for a wider image or show smaller lettering:

That choice belongs to the engine change that follows this decision. It does not affect which option to pick.

The trademark sign on the symbol, and small icons

The symbol-only logo needs a ™ too. It is placed just outside the circle at the lower right, about 4.5% of the symbol’s height, and is the same in every option.

Question 2: small icons

The six PNG icons are the symbol alone, from 256 px down to 16 px. Here is the symbol with its ™ at those sizes. At 32 px the sign is about one pixel tall, and at 16 px it is less than that. The choices are to add it to all six, add it to the larger sizes only, or leave the icons without it.

How we know today’s typeface is Raleway

Today’s lettering is stored as outlines, and the files do not record a font name. To identify it, we rendered the current banner and laid Raleway SemiBold over it at the same size.

Both agree Only in today’s logo Only in Raleway

The letter shapes coincide. What is left is a thin fringe a few pixels wide, and the whole line differs in length by 0.1%. The other Raleway weights, tested from 400 to 900, all matched clearly worse.

Raleway and Inter are both free under the SIL Open Font License. The final logo files will have their letters converted to outlines, so nobody needs either font installed to use them.

After the decision

  1. Produce the final SVG files with the letters converted to outlines, and the PNG icons if question 2 calls for a change.
  2. Open a pull request to the governance repository with those files.
  3. Update the copies of the logo on openintegrationengine.org. With option C or D, the website’s own font becomes a separate question.
  4. Update the copies in the engine repository: the Swing Administrator’s two 215 × 30 px banners (see the slot size above), its 32 px window icon, the 48 px icons, and the logos on the server’s landing page and API documentation page.
